## 1. Book facts

**Observed:**
- **Files:**
  - `Easter-Journey-Pages-qzyf3m.pdf`: 47 single pages (page 1 is the cover, then 46 interior pages).
  - `Easter-Journey-Spreads-ppiam1.pdf`: 24 pages (the cover, then 23 two-page spreads of 18×11 in).
  - Both were made in InDesign on Oct 15, 2025. The text uses EB Garamond and can be extracted with `pdftotext`.
- **Page mapping:**
  - Spreads PDF page k (k≥2) = Pages PDF pages 2k−2 and 2k−1.
  - The printed page number = Pages PDF page − 2. Printed "1" is the welcome page (Pages PDF p3); the last printed page is 45.
- **Trim size:** 9 × 11 in portrait (648×792 pt). The trim, bleed and media boxes are all the same size.
- **Live listing:** "Hardcover | 9″ x 11″ | 46 pages | Dust Jacket". The PDF download listing says 45 pages.
- **Credits:**
  - Written by Tiffany Wheeler (Tiffany Books LLC).
  - Illustrated by Kelley Hicken (Kelley Hicken Illustration).
  - "Copyright ©2025 Tiffany Books LLC and Kelley Hicken Illustration. All Rights Reserved."
  - The publisher is Tiffany Books LLC, so the book is self-published.
- **Not found anywhere:** no ISBN, Library of Congress number, printer or edition line in the PDFs. None of the live-site product data has an ISBN either. Ask the client.
- **Word count:** about 3,650 words of story (PDF pages 3–43), about 4,300 counting the back matter. A typical picture book is 500–1,000 words, so this is a long read-aloud.
- **Reading level:** Flesch-Kincaid grade 4.2, about 11.6 words per sentence.
- **Format:** The reader is a traveler in Jerusalem looking for Jesus. Eyewitnesses speak to "you" in first person, with speaker tags.
  - It is interactive, call-and-response: the reader answers "(We seek Jesus of Nazareth.)", "(Yes.)" and "(Shalom.)".
  - It works as reader's theater.

**Back matter:**
- "Can You Find These Symbols of Christ Throughout this Book?" (printed p42): cross, Easter lily, oil lamp, dove, fig, pelican, palm leaves, door knocker.
- "Easter Journey Advent" (printed p45): an 8-day guide from Sunday (Day One) to Sunday (Day Eight). Saturday is "Christ in the Tomb (No Reading)". Each day has discussion questions. A fig symbol behind the page number marks where each day's reading ends.

**Scripture source:** New Testament only, quoting the KJV. I saw no Book of Mormon content. There is some Latter-day Saint wording ("testify", "testimony", "I know that my Redeemer lives", Christ's blood in Gethsemane). The site copy's "higher and holier way" also sounds Latter-day Saint; check where it comes from before using it in ads.

**Characters:** Merchant (the guide who opens and closes the story), the man born blind, a Pharisee, Matthew, Andrew, James, John, Judas (mentioned), a Roman soldier, an unnamed woman believer, Caiaphas, Mary the mother of Jesus, the angel, Mary Magdalene, Jesus, and children in the finale.

**Themes:** seeking Jesus, Jesus as the promised Messiah and King, spiritual sight, love and service, standing up for your faith, trusting God in grief, the Resurrection, and "your role in the Easter story."

**Age estimate (inference):**
- About ages 5–12 as a family read-aloud; roughly grades 3–5 for reading alone. The puzzle box says "Ages 9+"; the book copy says "all ages".
- The story mentions betrayal, arrest, whipping, the crown of thorns and blood in Gethsemane. None of this is shown graphically, and the art never shows the crucifixion itself.

**Illustration style:** polished digital painting in a semi-realistic, animated-film look. Warm golden and teal lighting, expressive faces, full-bleed spreads, and text set on soft faded areas of the art.

## 4. Marketing hooks and companion products

**Hooks:**
- **Holy Week reading plan built in.** Read one part each day from Palm Sunday to Easter morning. This supports "order by X to start on Palm Sunday" deadline ads. For 2027, Palm Sunday is Mar 21 and Easter is Mar 28 (please double-check these dates).
- **Kids take part.** The call-and-response lines and speaker tags make it easy to act out for family night, Primary or Sunday school, and church groups.
- **Seek-and-find** of 8 symbols of Christ, plus discussion questions for each day.
- **Eyewitness voices.** About 12 people who knew Jesus tell the story, which makes it feel immersive.
- **Christ-centered Easter** as the alternative to the Easter bunny ("put Jesus in the Easter basket").
- **Wider audience than Nephite Christmas (inference).** The Easter book uses only the New Testament, so it can be marketed to Christians generally, not only Latter-day Saints.
- **Bundle story.** Nephite Christmas and Easter Journey pair as "His birth and His Resurrection."
