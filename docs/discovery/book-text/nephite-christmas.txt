'Twas the night before Christmas, and all through the land...
...the wicked were threatening, the time was at hand.
For Samuel the prophet said the sign would soon come, but the wait had just gotten too long for some.
The believers all hoped it would come any day. For the sign to appear, they continued to pray.
Though many great miracles had already occurred, the unbelievers continued to doubt Samuel's word.
A tremendous uproar soon began to arise, disbelievers now started to spread all their lies!
"The Savior's not coming; your faith is in vain. Quit praying and watching, you've nothing to gain!"
But the believers did watch with incredible faith, for they believed the words that Samuel had saith.
"You must wait for the day and the night that are one, a night without darkness, with no setting sun!"
Now the time had arrived, a day set apart to see who was really a believer at heart. The wicked said all the believers would die if the sign didn't come when evening was nigh.
So, Nephi, the prophet went out and bowed down in a small grove of trees on the outside of town.
He earnestly poured out his heart to the Lord that the faithful would not all now die by the sword.
And the Lord, who listens and answers our prayers, comforted the prophet and calmed all his fears. "Son, lift up your head, and be of good cheer! For the time is at hand, and the sign is so near!"
"On this very night shall the words be fulfilled, for I come to obey and do my Father's will.
For of Mary tomorrow I shall be born, in Bethlehem of Judea, that first Christmas morn."
So that glorious night, no darkness came.
The faithful were saved and the others ashamed.
There had been many who didn't believe, now they fell to earth - they had been deceived.
T'was the night before Christmas and the faithful were saved, because the sign came that foretold of the babe.
Asleep in a manger and wrapped in a shawl, the Savior, as prophesied, came to save all!
And so from the Nephites of old unto you, remember Christ came to save all of us, too! So never lose faith! Keep Him always in sight! Merry Christmas to all, and to all a good night!
