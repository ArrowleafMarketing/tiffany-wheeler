##### Tiffany Wheeler
## My Story
 Hi, I’m Tiffany Wheeler. If we were sitting together around the breakfast table, I’d probably start by telling you about my family. I live in Idaho’s beautiful Treasure Valley with my husband, John, and we have six incredible children. Family is my greatest joy and the place where I learn and grow the most. When I’m not gathering people together, you’ll probably find me kayaking on a mountain lake, hiking somewhere with a view, planning my next trip, or curled up with a good book.
I’ve loved books for as long as I can remember. As a child, I spent my summers biking to the library, filling my basket with books, and reading beneath the lilac bushes until dinnertime. Stories such as Little Women, The Count of Monte Cristo, and To Kill a Mockingbird taught me that a good book can shape our character and help us see the world differently. Now, as the author of Nephite Christmas and Easter Journey, I love creating faith-filled stories and experiences that bring families closer to one another and to Jesus Christ.
In 2022, breast cancer caused me to pause and think about what I wanted to do with my life. During that time, a friend gave me a journal with the words, “What the caterpillar calls the end of the world, the Master calls the butterfly.” That message encouraged me to uncover talents I had buried and trust God to help me become something more. It’s also why there’s a small butterfly in my logo—it reminds me where my wings come from.
I always have a new book or business idea floating around in my head, and I am excited for one of my new ventures. After years of serving youth and working in suicide and addiction prevention, I’ve become convinced that small moments of genuine connection can change lives. That belief inspired Connection Crates—creating connection tools for families- tools that can be delivered to your front door- so you actually use them. Through simple activities, practical tools, and meaningful conversations, Connection Crates helps families slow down, laugh together, and build stronger relationships one small moment at a time. This is also going to be part of the next book series that I am working on to help youth and children understand the value of Connection and how to find and create more connection in their lives.
The other project I am working on, touches deep on my faith and love for my Savior. All things point to Him. That is how Easter Journey started. But teaching early-morning seminary also helped me discover how much joy comes from applying gospel principles—not just learning them. This year, I’m launching ACT: Applying Christ’s Teachings, a podcast centered on one simple idea: the gospel isn’t something we only learn; it’s something we live. As we act on what we’re learning, we begin to recognize Jesus Christ in our everyday choices, relationships, and experiences. That’s where true joy is found.
My mantra is “Enjoy the Journey.” I believe life is meant to be lived with passion! Passion for your faith, your family, your friendships, your life and the beautiful things of this world! That’s why I love writing books and creating things that bring people closer together and closer to Christ. He is the source of my joy and my strength.
I’m so glad our paths have crossed. I hope something I create brings a little more faith, connection, and joy to your journey!

# Welcome to Easter Journey
Welcome, my friends! I am so excited you’re joining us as we bring [->https://tiffany-wheeler.com/product/easter-journey/] Easter Journey to life.
What started in 2007 as a small Easter event in our church—to help families and our community experience Easter together—became more powerful and meaningful than I ever imagined. Originally created as a play, Easter Journey has since evolved into a tool to help families celebrate Easter in a higher, holier way.
Based on the stories from the Gospels, this story is made to be shared by Christians of every denomination.
 [->https://tiffany-wheeler.com/product/easter-journey/] [Preorder Your Copy Today →] Orders will ship beginning February 1st.
## Experience Easter in a Higher and Holier Way
Through Easter Journey, your family can experience Easter in a higher, holier way—feel Christ’s love, and begin traditions that draw you closer to Him year after year. It’s not just a story—it’s an experience, a tradition, and a chance for all of us to truly come closer to Christ.
## How It Began
The first year we did the play, about a thousand people walked through the story of Christ in our building. Each year, more came, and we added nights and space to accommodate everyone.
It wasn’t about fancy sets or special effects—it was about immersing people in the story of Jesus, helping them feel like they were really there.
One 10-year-old told me he wanted to go through it again and again, saying it was even better than Disneyland. The story came alive, and the Spirit testified of Christ in ways words alone cannot describe.
## Why it Matters
For years, families have asked if they could get a copy of Easter Journey to share at home. Inspired by a faith leader’s invitation to celebrate Easter in a “higher and holier” way, I knew it was time to make this experience available to more families.
We need to up our game when it comes to putting Christ back into Easter.
 [->https://tiffany-wheeler.com/product/easter-journey/] [Preorder Your Copy Today →] Orders will ship beginning February 1st. 
## What is Easter Journey?
Easter Journey is more than just a book—it is truly an experience.
As you take the Easter Journey, you will be welcomed into Jerusalem and encouraged to go into the city to seek Jesus of Nazareth. Along the way, you will hear of His miracles, listen to His teachings, and learn for yourself if He truly is the Messiah that was prophesied to come.
You’ll hear from the merchant whose heart was touched by His teachings, the blind man who was healed, the apostles sharing the sacrament, and the women who witnessed His resurrection. You will hear His mother testify of His divine calling, and you will even face Caiaphas and be invited to ask yourself: Am I willing to stand up for Jesus?
## Ways to Experience Easter Journey
One of the beautiful things about Easter Journey is its versatility. You can:
- Read it together as a story
- Use it as a reader’s theater
- Enjoy it as an advent leading up to Easter
It includes discussion questions in the back and a fun seek-and-find for the symbols of Christ throughout. It works for any age and level of your family.
## The Ongoing Story
The greatest part of Easter Journey is realizing that the Easter story isn’t over—it’s ongoing. Each of us has a role to play.
Christ came and died for YOU! Just like each of the people you meet along the way, you are also an important part of the Easter story. You can continue to witness of Christ’s teachings and miracles in your own life.
Shalom!
